Where we focus

Ideas that move people forward.

Our work centres on youth engagement, digital learning and community collaboration — creating spaces where young people can learn, connect and act.

How we work

Learn, connect, act.

Our approach moves through three stages: encounter new ideas, build meaningful connections, and translate understanding into practical action.

01

Learn

Spaces to encounter ideas, ask questions and grow in understanding.

02

Connect

Relationships across communities that strengthen perspective and purpose.

03

Act

Practical opportunities to contribute, serve and shape community.

Our programmes

Initiatives that shape young people and community.

The following are focus areas currently reflected in our work. Formal programme names, dates and details will be confirmed and updated as initiatives are formally approved.

Community engagement

Youth & Purpose

Intended audience: young adults and students.

Overview

Creating space for young people to reflect on purpose, relationships, culture and the choices that shape their lives — through conversations, gatherings and shared learning.

Key activities

  • — Conversations on faith, purpose and culture
  • — Small-group discussions and reflections
  • — Peer-led gatherings and community time

Intended outcomes

Greater clarity of purpose, stronger peer connections and a sense of belonging within a meaningful community.

Learning & innovation

Digital Learning

Intended audience: students, emerging professionals.

Overview

Exploring digital literacy, emerging technology and learning opportunities that can help young people prepare for tomorrow — from AI to blockchain, design to digital tools.

Key activities

  • — Workshops on emerging technologies
  • — Conference-style learning gatherings
  • — Resources and skill-building sessions

Intended outcomes

Improved digital confidence, awareness of emerging technologies and practical readiness to engage with the future of work.

Flagship event: Digital for Impact 1.0 — 21 November 2026.

Collective action

Community & Collaboration

Intended audience: contributors, partners, volunteers.

Overview

Bringing people and perspectives together to share knowledge, strengthen connections and work toward the common good of communities.

Key activities

  • — Volunteer mobilisation for community events
  • — Partnerships with schools, organisations and groups
  • — Collaborative projects and shared initiatives

Intended outcomes

Stronger networks, shared purpose across communities and tangible contributions to the people around us.

These are descriptive focus themes rather than a complete or formally approved programme list. Confirmed programme names, dates and details will replace these placeholders once approved.
