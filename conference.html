Conference overview

Technology. People. Possibility.

Join a day of ideas and dialogue about building a more innovative and inclusive future. We will explore how emerging technologies — especially AI and blockchain — can be used not just to impress, but to equip, to connect, and to create meaningful impact in our communities.

This is a gathering for anyone who believes technology should serve people — and who wants to be part of shaping how it does.

Purpose & objectives

Why this conference matters.

01

Equip young people

Introduce practical understanding of AI and blockchain so participants can engage with confidence.

02

Spark meaningful conversation

Create space for dialogue on how technology can serve community, faith and purpose.

03

Connect people & ideas

Bring together students, professionals, technologists and community contributors.

04

Encourage responsible innovation

Explore how emerging tools can be used ethically and for the common good.

05

Inspire action

Encourage participants to take practical next steps toward building and contributing.

06

Build community

Strengthen a network of young people exploring technology, faith and impact together.

Intended audience

Who should attend?

Anyone curious about how technology can be used for good — whether you are just starting out or already working in the field.

Students & young adults

Curious about AI, blockchain and where technology is heading.

Emerging professionals

Looking to understand emerging technologies and their practical use.

Technologists & builders

Interested in how their skills can create meaningful community impact.

Community contributors

Wanting to understand and support youth-led digital initiatives.

Topics & discussion areas

What we will explore together.

Artificial Intelligence

What AI is, how it works, and how it can be used responsibly to learn, build and create value.

Blockchain & Web3

Understanding the foundations of blockchain and what it means for trust, ownership and community.

Digital leadership

How young people can lead with purpose in an age of rapid technological change.

Building & innovation

Turning ideas into practical projects — from concept to community impact.

Technology & faith

How Christian values can shape the way we build, use and govern technology.

Community impact

Practical ways technology can strengthen communities and create shared progress.
